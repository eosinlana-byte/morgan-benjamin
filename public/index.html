ABOUT MORGAN

The music comes first.

Morgan Benjamin is a singer and songwriter working somewhere between R&B, Afrobeats, soul, and pop. His songs are built around real moments, relationships, late nights, things left unsaid, and everything that happens in between.

He started writing songs as a way to make sense of things he couldn't always say out loud. Over time, those private moments became records, performances, and a sound of his own.

No big story. Just good songs and something worth saying.
